Privacy ≠ Security

🔒 Security

Protects accounts and systems from unauthorised access: strong unique passwords, MFA, login alerts, patched devices.

🙈 Privacy

Controls what personal information is collected, shared and exposed: profile visibility, location sharing, tags, public posts.

Example: an account can have a strong password and MFA (good security) yet publish your phone number, birthday and live location to everyone (poor privacy). Strong account security ≠ strong privacy.

Privacy by design: what this app stores

✔ Stored (minimal)

✘ Never stored or asked

Phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, birth dates, passwords, exact locations, messages, or even your individual answers.

⚠ This is an educational risk framework based on self-reported answers. It is not a guarantee that an account will or will not be compromised. Weights and thresholds are educational assumptions.
