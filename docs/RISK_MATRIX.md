# Privacy Risk Matrix

Likelihood and impact are **educational estimates**; real risk depends on context (who you are, who might target you, what else is public).

| Finding | Likelihood | Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profile is publicly visible | High | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Profile can appear in search engines | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Friends / followers list is visible | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Account can be found by phone / email look-up | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Same username across platforms | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Phone number reported as publicly visible | Medium | Medium | IMMEDIATE |
| Personal email reported as publicly visible | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Full birth date is publicly visible | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Home-related details are publicly visible | Medium | High | IMMEDIATE |
| Workplace is publicly visible | Medium | Medium | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Education is publicly visible | Low | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Family / relationship details are public | Medium | Medium | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Location shown on public profile | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Real-time location sharing enabled | Medium | High | IMMEDIATE |
| Posts carry location tags | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Live check-ins while at a place | Medium | High | IMPORTANT |
| Travel plans posted before / during trips | Medium | High | IMPORTANT |
| Posts reveal routines | Medium | Medium | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Posts are publicly visible by default | High | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Photos show badges, documents or plates | Medium | High | IMMEDIATE |
| Public photos of children / family | Medium | High | IMMEDIATE |
| Stories / reels visible outside connections | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Anyone can send connection requests | High | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Unknown connection requests accepted | High | Medium | IMMEDIATE |
| Connections not reviewed recently | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| No close-friends / restricted lists used | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Tag review is disabled | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Anyone can tag you | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Tagged posts appear automatically | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Anyone can mention you | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Multi-factor authentication is disabled | Medium | High | IMMEDIATE |
| MFA method is weak or unknown | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Password reuse reported | High | High | IMMEDIATE |
| No password manager in use | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Login alerts are disabled | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Active sessions not reviewed | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Recovery information not reviewed | Medium | High | IMPORTANT |
| Unknown devices not checked | Medium | Medium | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Third-party apps not reviewed | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Unused connected apps remain | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| App permissions not checked | Medium | Medium | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Heavy use of 'Sign in with social account' | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Replies to suspicious messages | High | Medium | IMMEDIATE |
| Low confidence spotting scam links | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Clicks unexpected links without checking | High | High | IMMEDIATE |
| Verification codes shared on request | Medium | High | IMMEDIATE |
| No verification of urgent requests from friends | High | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Joins giveaways asking for logins or forms | Medium | Medium | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Old public posts not reviewed | Medium | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Privacy settings not reviewed recently | High | Medium | IMPORTANT |
| Old or unused accounts still active | Medium | Medium | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Photo metadata not managed | Medium | Medium | GOOD PRACTICE |
| Personal details in public comments | Medium | Low | GOOD PRACTICE |
